Hatch a pet

Burn play XCPETS with the tag pet:hatch. The pet is rolled from a fresh random fake block hash and burn id by the same generator as the real game, so species odds, rarity tiers and gear are the real ones. The burn amount becomes its first care, and in this demo the 69 minimum hatches it straight to Hatchling, so rerolling for a rarer pet is cheap. Pets start with no gear: you roll gear for the slots they unlock.

Bulk buys: a dozen is 12 eggs for the price of 10; a case is a gross (144) for 6,900, about 31% off. Every egg is its own normal hatch.

My pets

How to upgrade: tap the big Feed button on a pet to reach its next stage. Each stage unlocks a gear slot. Pick an unlocked slot and tap Roll (345) to roll an item for it (empty slots equip it right away); rolls get better as the pet grows. Stage-ups (25%) and feeds of 690+ (2%) can drop a free roll token, and every roll has a 1% chance of a Slot Key that unlocks a bonus slot (face, then back, then feet) for meme gear. At Elder, tap Evolve for Mythic. Out of play money? Tap Refill.

Demo rules: the 69 hatch makes a Hatchling, then Juvenile at 10,419 care (+10,350), Adult at 24,219 (+13,800), Elder at 41,469 (+17,250). At Elder, an evolve burn of 27,531 (pet:evolve:ID) makes it Mythic. Egg to Mythic: 69,000 play XCPETS. Slots unlock by stage: band at Hatchling, armor at Juvenile, cape at Adult, weapon at Elder, crown at Mythic. Roll odds by stage: Hatchling Common only; Juvenile 80/20; Adult 70/22/8; Elder 68/22/8/2; Mythic 60/25/11/4 (Common/Uncommon/Rare/Legendary). Bonus gear: Counterparty coin (Common), HODL banner and Stack of sats (Uncommon), Paper-hands cape and Hard-wallet amulet (Rare), Beam eyes (Legendary); if a rolled rarity has no item in that bonus slot, the nearest lower one is used, else the slot's lowest. A gear roll is a play burn, so it also feeds the shared pet.
